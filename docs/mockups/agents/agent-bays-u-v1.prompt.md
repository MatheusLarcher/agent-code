# Mockup das baias dos agentes em U

Imagem: `agent-bays-u-v1.png`.

Mockup visual gerado com a ferramenta integrada imagegen. O exemplo tem 12 baias: quatro ao fundo e quatro em cada lateral. O U abre para a câmera, com todas as faces dos monitores visíveis. Esta imagem serve para revisar o layout do escritório.

## Prompt utilizado

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: visual layout mockup for the Agent Code virtual 3D agents office.
Primary request: Render an office with agent work bays arranged in a clear U shape, open toward the viewer. Every monitor screen must face the viewer so the user can see all screens at the same time.
Scene: a refined miniature office diorama with a warm gray floor and low pale walls in the background. The U arrangement is the main subject, with a large completely empty central viewing aisle opening toward the bottom of the image.
Layout and viewing geometry: 12 individual workstation bays: 4 across the rear horizontal bar, 4 along the left arm, and 4 along the right arm. The two side arms extend toward the viewer with clear separation from the rear desks. Desk positions form a squared horseshoe / U, absolutely NOT a rectangular grid or closed ring. Use the camera positioned above the middle of the U opening, looking straight at its rear bar, an elevated front perspective at roughly 40 degrees. Orient EACH monitor independently toward this camera, including all 8 side-arm monitors: their luminous FRONT faces must be clearly visible, never their backs, never edge-on. Side screens should be slightly diagonally rotated toward the lower-center viewing point. Stagger or space the bays enough that none of the 12 screens overlaps another.
Workstations: clean contemporary light wood desks, dark thin-bezel desktop monitors, keyboards, ergonomic chairs, short low privacy fins dividing individual bays. Screen size large relative to each desk for readability. Distinct realistic-looking dark UI content on the 12 screens: colorful code editor syntax, terminal output, a web page preview, tests and a small dashboard. Content is just visual texture, no long text. Each screen is tilted gently upward to face the elevated camera.
Agents: one small human 3D cartoon developer per desk, wearing varied casual professional clothes. Soft sculpted anatomy, detailed cloth textures, natural proportions, friendly faces, refined premium toy-diorama rendering. Seated agents should be low enough and slightly offset so their heads and chair backs do not obscure any monitor. Do not let people or furniture block screen faces.
Style: high-quality 3D miniature office game mockup, polished PBR materials, soft global illumination, fine ambient occlusion, subtle warm/cool contrast, clean restrained decor, clear spatial organization.
Composition: wide landscape 16:9, symmetric U-shaped arrangement, fully framed and generous central negative space. Emphasize the open U silhouette and simultaneous visibility of ALL 12 screens. No unrelated furniture inside the central aisle, no roof, no tall partitions, no glass reflections obstructing screens. No infographic, no arrows, no text labels, no logos or watermark. Produce a single finished concept image.
```
